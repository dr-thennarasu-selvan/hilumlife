HILUM LIFE is building Sthira, an AI app that tracks lung health continuously against your own baseline using your wearable device, and tells you when it is time to see your doctor.

Lung function declines silently

People with chronic lung disease usually find out something is wrong only when breathlessness forces them to hospital. By then, a problem that could have been handled early has become an emergency.

Sthira: continuous, personal lung-health monitoring

Using your wearable device, Sthira turns everyday signals into a lung-health picture for one person, so changes are spotted early, not at the hospital door.

Wear

Sthira tracks your lungs using your wearable device. No clinic visit needed.

Learn

Sthira builds your personal lung-health baseline.

Watch and check

It spots meaningful change from your baseline and checks in with you before raising an alert.

Act early

It tells you when it is time to see your doctor.

Who it is for

  • People aged 50 and above living with COPD, asthma or other chronic lung disease
  • Their families, who usually make the hospital trip with them
  • Anyone who wants to keep an eye on their lung health over time

What it is, and is not

  • A screening and early-warning tool that tells you when to see a doctor
  • Built by a pulmonologist, around how lung disease actually behaves
  • Not a diagnosis, and not a replacement for clinic lung tests or your doctor's care
